## Purpose

Screen postings inexpensively and evaluate admitted jobs with accountable native tools while retaining canonical evidence, eligibility and human authority.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Deterministic screening before paid job assessment
The system SHALL apply approved candidate policy and current digest through deterministic seniority/experience, location/remote/available-distance and work-rights/hours checks before paid per-job assessment. Clear exclusions SHALL stop with an auditable reason and reversible override; unknowns SHALL remain explicit.

#### Scenario: Clear seniority mismatch
- **WHEN** an explicitly senior posting exceeds the candidate's approved supported levels
- **THEN** the job is skipped without a per-job model call

#### Scenario: Ambiguous or exceptional condition
- **WHEN** wording is negated, optional, contradictory, missing or subject to an applicable exception
- **THEN** the system preserves the uncertainty or exception rather than declaring unsupported incompatibility

### Requirement: Native evidence-bound evaluation
Admitted jobs SHALL use native tool calling to produce canonical evaluation directly, without the old multi-role evaluator or an added model supervisor. Requirements SHALL be frozen before candidate matching; canonical source citations, coverage and eligibility SHALL remain enforced.

#### Scenario: Direct native assessment
- **WHEN** an admitted posting is evaluated
- **THEN** whole-ad requirements, evidence retrieval and justified assessment produce a canonical gate outcome without document generation or submission authority

### Requirement: Consistent completion and targeted recovery
Completion tools SHALL enforce the same relevant shape and content constraints as canonical output. Recoverable schema/citation errors SHALL receive bounded field-specific feedback without weakening evidence requirements or restarting completed candidate work.

#### Scenario: Summary exceeds canonical section size
- **WHEN** a completion section exceeds the canonical limit
- **THEN** the completion is not acknowledged as successful and feedback identifies the affected field before final assembly

#### Scenario: Claimed quote is absent
- **WHEN** an asserted citation cannot resolve to its source
- **THEN** the citation is rejected and correction must supply valid evidence or an allowed unknown state

### Requirement: Accountable recovery and measurements
The system SHALL report calls by purpose, known token usage including available reasoning/cache details, unknown charges and elapsed time. It SHALL preserve completed paid work on unchanged-input resume, invalidate changed bindings, and honor configured owner limits without converting the 3–6-call efficiency target into a hidden cap.

#### Scenario: Resume after persistence interruption
- **WHEN** a successful paid response was journaled before a later interruption
- **THEN** unchanged-input recovery reuses that response without a second paid dispatch

#### Scenario: Efficiency target is missed
- **WHEN** measured job assessment exceeds six model calls
- **THEN** the result reports actual calls and cause separately from classifier/embedding operations and digest cost without claiming target success

### Requirement: Deployment evidence
Activation SHALL require required local checks and a controlled valid provider outcome. Completion SHALL identify the exact live native revision, effective evaluator/protocol and observed canonical outcome, with rollback preserving new data and accounting.

#### Scenario: Source checks pass but provider fails
- **WHEN** synthetic checks pass but canonical provider acceptance fails
- **THEN** assessment remains paused and the release is reported blocked rather than deployed as working
