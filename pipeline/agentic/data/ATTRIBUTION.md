# Metropolitan locality reference

Factual locality-to-council mappings from the State of Victoria, Know Your Council. Source URLs and SHA256 hashes for79 pages are recorded in metro-localities.json. Retrieved11October2026. Metro council membership is from https://liveinmelbourne.vic.gov.au/discover/melbourne/metropolitan-melbourne (web observation; direct fetch403, not bypassed).

Extraction: read each council page Localities section; split comma lists and final conjunction; remove explicit(part) suffix; for Benalla remove the two north/south introductory phrases before splitting. Casefold for matching. Preserve source spellings; no guessed aliases or postcodes. Any name also listed by a regional council is ambiguous and cannot independently establish metro eligibility. This is a locality-name reference, not parcel-level GIS or complete current locality coverage. Unknown and ambiguous places hold for verification.

Original government content: https://www.vic.gov.au/copyright. No personal candidate data is included. Updating facts requires source verification; the exact reference bytes participate in decision identity.
