name: autonomous-evaluation
description: Coordinate Screening then Evaluation for one immutable saved posting.
---

Use task with subagent_type screening first, then evaluation for admit or flag.
The evaluation specialist extracts the whole ad without candidate context, retrieves
primary evidence for frozen requirements, and produces a separate advisory summary.
Those specialists use native tools and exact canonical schemas. Their validators
own citations, retrieved evidence and requirement identity. Do not fill gaps from
memory, scores or previous conversations. Missing facts remain unknown. Do not run
stages in parallel. Stop once the canonical evaluation is saved; the caller applies
the canonical eligibility gate. No documents, employer messages, profile confirmation
or application submission are available in this workflow.
