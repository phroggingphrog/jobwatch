name: prepare-application
description: Prepare a tailored CV, cover letter and application form map for a saved job.
---

Inspect the saved ad and latest assessment. Use company_researcher to inspect the complete ad, desired skills and cited public company context. Load company-research first. The canonical writer also gathers this research directly; a chat brief is not its only input.
Connect verified company work and actual duties to the person's expressed career interests and supported experience for a natural why-this-job paragraph. Never turn company values or a model preference score into invented personal beliefs.
Request human approval for prepare_application. This invokes the existing evidence-bound evaluator, writer, factual reviewer, PDF and form-map checks.
Canonical profile facts come from the person's evidence files; advisory memory cannot replace them.
Only factual/company/placeholder/document-integrity failures block. Voice and taste feedback permits one rewrite, never an endless rejection loop.
Report returned status, reason and artifact references. A form map is not a filled browser form. Preparing never submits; final Submit is the owner's click or an approved `submit_application` for the reviewed fill.
