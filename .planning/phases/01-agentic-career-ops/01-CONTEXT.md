# Phase 01 — agentic career-ops context

## Goal / authority
Bounded Career Ops evaluation, optional research, tailored drafting and fresh review, retaining deterministic collection, persistence and routing. Repository-root DESIGN-agentic.md is the authoritative Discuss design: decisions, alternatives, defaults, constraints, acceptance. Read it, GOAL_CONTRACT.md and reference/{oferta,_shared}.md.

## Decisions and observable must-haves
- Pipeline-launched evaluator/researcher + drafter/reviser on openai/sol; fresh reviewer on openai/astra. Existing OmniRoute client, explicit bounded tool choice; no new service or Paperclip agent mutations.
- Full ad and JD-first requirement importance, then primary CV/evidence matching. Owner career targets supersede archived senior-AI archetypes. Holistic five-dimension global score is versioned separately from legacy arithmetic.
- Code enforces hard gates and >=4.0 drafting; re-evaluate backlog before drafting; rejected/held work never becomes READY to hit a quota.
- Reuse draft/ledger/render/verify_pdf/form-map/board interfaces. Leases/CAS/reconciliation prevent duplicate work; actors truthfully identify automation. Candidate material never enters git/logs/issues.
- Synthetic safety/restart/budget/flag-off tests and full pytest pass; after authorized live execution >=20 real qualifying jobs have accepted reviews, verified CV+letter, form maps and board READY. Unknown answers remain blank and visibly marked. Design enumerates exact checks.

## Scope and defaults
In: bounded runner, structured outputs, safe employer research, pipeline/progress integration, tests, bounded backlog and evidence/docs. Out: collector redesign, archive service restore, form typing/submission/contact, new subscriptions, independent T19. [default] two concurrent jobs, batches of 20, strict turn/time/token ceilings in design. Reported 126 is historical; snapshot actual eligible IDs at execution.

## Handoff
Staff approves DESIGN-agentic.md before implementation. Eng Manager creates <=3-task fresh-context plans, disjoint files per wave; obtains plan check. Then creates Build, QA Verify and Release issues with first-class blockers. Release obtains board authorization for merge/restart/enabling/live data writes. Parent completion requires full acceptance, not this Discuss document.
